NflCardDB page grabber

Grabs the sold listings off an eBay page you are looking at.

Install it once

Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show your bookmarks bar, then drag the blue button onto it.

Grab card sales

Dragging is required -- clicking it here does nothing, because there are no eBay listings on this page.

Use it

  1. Search eBay as you normally would, signed in as yourself.
  2. In the left sidebar, tick Sold items.
  3. At the bottom of the results, set the page size to 240 -- that makes each grab worth 240 sales instead of 60.
  4. Click Grab card sales on your bookmarks bar. A small .json file downloads.
  5. Drag that file onto import.bat.

Repeat for as many pages as you want. Duplicates are ignored, so you can never double-count a sale by grabbing the same page twice.

Why this works when the collector does not

Nothing here contacts eBay. You load the page yourself, in your own browser, signed in as yourself -- and this only reads what is already on your screen. There is no automated request for eBay to refuse.
